Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Account and Data Deletion Request

Application: Sudoku: Logic Puzzles (com.slaboratory.sudoku)
Developer: sLaboratory
Last Updated: September 11, 2026

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy and global privacy standards (GDPR, CCPA), users of Sudoku: Logic Puzzles have the right to permanently delete their user accounts and all associated data stored in our cloud databases.

How to Delete Your Account and Data

Method 1: Directly Inside the Application (Instant)

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the game at any time without contacting support:

  1. Open Sudoku: Logic Puzzles on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the top navigation bar.
  3. Select Account & Cloud Sync.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm your choice. Your account credentials and cloud-synced records will be purged immediately.
Method 2: Request Deletion via Email (Web / Outside App)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request to our support team:

  • Send an email to: support@slaboratory.com
  • Subject line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Sudoku: Logic Puzzles
  • Body: Please provide the email address associated with your Google or Apple Sign-In account (or your anonymous User ID if known).
Request Deletion via Email

Our technical support team will process your request and permanently purge all associated records within 30 days.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently purged from our servers:

  • Authentication Records: Your Firebase Auth account, unique User ID (UID), and linked Google/Apple account references.
  • Cloud Game Saves & Progress: All synced puzzle solutions, chapter completion stars, unlocked techniques, and custom constraints saved in Cloud Firestore.
  • Multiplayer Data: Duel history, match room logs, and competitive rankings in Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Virtual Currency Ledger: In-game soft currency (Blue Crystals) history.

What Data May Be Retained

Financial and In-App Purchase Records: Purchase receipts processed through Google Play In-App Billing or RevenueCat may be retained strictly for tax compliance, anti-fraud auditing, and statutory financial reporting as required by applicable laws. These records do not contain personal gameplay history.

Local Device Storage: Deleting your cloud account does not automatically delete locally cached SQLite data on your phone. To remove all local data, simply clear the app storage in your Android device settings or uninstall the app.
