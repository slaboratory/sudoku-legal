Privacy Policy

Application: Sudoku: Logic Puzzles (Package: com.slaboratory.sudoku)
Developer: sLaboratory
Effective Date: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how sLaboratory ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application Sudoku: Logic Puzzles (the "Application" or "Game").

We designed Sudoku: Logic Puzzles with an offline-first philosophy. You can enjoy the vast majority of puzzles, custom variants, and campaign chapters without providing personal data or maintaining an active internet connection.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide gameplay features, cross-device synchronization, fair multiplayer duels, and technical stability.

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Authentication Details: By default, you can play anonymously without providing any credentials. If you choose to link your account to safeguard your progress or transfer it across devices, we collect your authentication credentials via Firebase Authentication (such as your Google Sign-In or Apple ID identifier, display name, and email address).
  • Support Requests: If you contact us via email for customer support or feedback, we retain your email address and communication history to resolve your query.

B. Automatically Collected Gameplay & Technical Data

  • Game Progress & Records: Finished boards, solved techniques, chapter stars, daily puzzle records, and in-game soft currency ledgers are saved locally on your device (via SQLite/Drift) and synced to our secure cloud database (Firebase Firestore) upon game events.
  • Multiplayer Duel Data: Real-time match data (room codes, moves, timestamps, and match results) processed via Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Anti-Cheat Verification Hashes: Game action sequences are validated using deterministic cryptographic hash chains (SHA-256) evaluated by secure cloud functions to guarantee leaderboard integrity and fair play.
  • Diagnostics and Crash Reports: Information about device model, operating system version, unique installation identifiers, and crash stack traces collected via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose technical defects.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable telemetry regarding in-game navigation, session duration, and feature usage collected via Firebase Analytics to improve gameplay balancing.

C. Financial and Purchase Information

All financial transactions (purchasing Pro subscriptions or virtual currency packs) are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing (for Android) or the Apple App Store (for iOS). We never collect, receive, or store your credit card or banking details.

We use RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts and manage subscription statuses. RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers, transaction IDs, product identifiers, and subscription expiration timestamps.

We partner with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the Application. These providers only access your information to perform specific tasks on our behalf in compliance with privacy regulations:

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • To enable core game mechanics, offline puzzles, and custom constraint solvers;
  • To synchronize progress across your authorized devices;
  • To facilitate real-time multiplayer duels and verify competitive leaderboard scores;
  • To grant and verify purchased Pro features and virtual items;
  • To identify bugs, prevent crashes, and optimize resource usage across device configurations;
  • To prevent fraud, automated bots, and tampering.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your gameplay and authentication data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the Game services.

Right to Delete Your Account and Data:
You can delete your account and associated cloud data at any time directly through the in-game Settings menu (Settings → Account → Delete Account), or by sending an email request to support@slaboratory.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Upon request, all personally identifiable account data and cloud-synced progress records will be permanently removed from our databases within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Sudoku: Logic Puzzles is a general-audience logic game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that personal information has been collected from a child under the applicable age without verifiable parental consent, we will promptly delete that information from our servers.

6. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and electronic security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All cloud communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access and Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your personal data ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent to data collection at any time by disconnecting linked authentication or deleting the app.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@slaboratory.com.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and, where appropriate, through an in-app notice.
